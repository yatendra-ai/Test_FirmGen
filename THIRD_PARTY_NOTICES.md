# Provenance

`generated/` and `user_modifiable/Device/STM32C5A3ZGT6/` contain STM32CubeC5-derived
board configuration/startup code. Original copyright notices and accompanying license
files are retained. Configuration and linker changes are project-specific.

Application/customer-driver portions are a synthetic testing fixture adapted from the
local PWM lab, not evidence of a shipped customer product. Identity values are fictitious.

The external STM32CubeC5 package is not redistributed in this archive. Use your installed
package and its applicable license terms. Neither hardware qualification nor certification
is claimed by a successful build.
